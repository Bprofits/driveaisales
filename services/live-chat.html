How It Runs At Your Store.

During the build we wire the chat to your inventory feed and calendar, set its boundaries with you, and tune the voice until it sounds like your best greeter. It answers what it knows, escalates what it does not, and never makes up a number.

  • Connected to your live inventory, so answers reflect what is actually on the lot.
  • Trade-in guidance uses your rules: ranges you approve, appraisal always the final word.
  • Boundaries set with you: what it may answer and what always goes to a human.
  • AMVIC and OMVIC advertising rules configured in, so chat never quotes what it should not.
  • Full transcripts logged to your CRM with the visitor's contact details and vehicle of interest.

Straight Answers.

Will it make up prices or promises?
No. It answers from your inventory feed and your approved rules, and anything outside them gets escalated to a human instead of guessed at. If it is unsure, it says so and books the conversation with your team. An honest "let me get you a person" beats a made-up number every time.
Can my team take over a live chat?
Yes. Your team can watch conversations, jump in mid-thread and take over completely, any time. The visitor sees one continuous conversation. The AI hands off with the transcript, so nobody asks the customer to repeat themselves.
How is this different from the chat widget my website already has?
Most widgets collect an email and promise a callback. This one answers the actual question, estimates the trade, books the test drive and logs the whole exchange in your CRM, at 2 PM or 2 AM. The difference is a booked appointment instead of a lead your team has to chase in the morning.
