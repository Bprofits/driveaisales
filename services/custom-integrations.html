How It Runs At Your Store.

Integration is the first conversation of your build, not an afterthought. We inventory your stack on the discovery call, tell you plainly what connects cleanly, what needs custom work, and what a platform simply will not allow, then build in that order.

  • Standard connections, common CRMs, calendars, phone systems and inventory feeds, are quick and included.
  • Direct integrations depend on what each platform allows. We never promise around an API that does not exist.
  • Some custom integrations carry additional API cost. You hear it on the discovery call, not on an invoice.
  • Everything lands in one hub: one inbox, one pipeline, one report.
  • Your data stays yours: 100 percent dealership ownership, exportable any time, PIPEDA and Alberta PIPA compliant.

Straight Answers.

Is every integration guaranteed?
No, and we would rather tell you that here than surprise you later. Direct integrations depend on what each platform allows: standard connections are quick and included, some need custom API work that carries additional cost, and a few things a closed platform simply will not permit. We scope all of it with you on the discovery call, before you commit.
Do I have to leave my current CRM or DMS?
No. Drive AI is a complete CRM in its own right, but plenty of stores start by connecting their existing systems and letting the data flow into one place. Migrate later, or never. The point is one source of truth, not a forced switch.
What does an integration project actually look like?
It is part of your build, not a separate project. We inventory the stack on the discovery call, confirm what each platform's API allows, and wire the connections in during the roughly 90-day rollout, standard ones first, custom ones scoped and priced before work starts.
