How It Runs At Your Store.

During the build we connect your calendars, define who takes what kind of appointment, and set the reminder cadence and wording with you. Then the machine runs it identically for every single booking, including the ones made at 2 AM.

  • Booked into your real calendar with rep assignment and prep buffers respected.
  • Reminder wording approved by you, in your store's voice, per appointment type.
  • Reschedule replies handled automatically: the slot moves, the buyer never falls through a crack.
  • No-show recovery cadence configured to your taste, from gentle to persistent.
  • Every appointment, reminder and outcome logged on the contact in your CRM.

Straight Answers.

Where does the 41% to 68% number come from?
41% is the industry-average show rate for booked dealership appointments. 68% is what disciplined confirmation plus night-before and morning-of reminders produces. The mechanism is boring and it works: people show up for things they were reminded of. We configure that discipline to run on every booking automatically.
What happens when somebody replies to a reminder?
The reply is read and acted on. A reschedule request gets two fresh slots and rebooks itself. A question gets answered. Anything that needs a human routes to your team with the thread attached. Reminders are a conversation, not a broadcast.
Can my team still book appointments manually?
Of course. Anything your reps book by hand gets the same confirmation, reminders and no-show recovery as an automated booking. The system does not care who created the appointment, it just makes sure the customer shows.
