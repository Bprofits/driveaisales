Straight Answers.

Is this actually reliable, or just a fancy guess?
It is a model, and we are straight about what that means: it reads pipeline health, response performance and your own historical pace, and shows you its inputs alongside the read. It will not predict a hailstorm. What it does reliably is flag a soft month early, while the flag is still useful, and name the deals that change it.
What do managers actually do with the forecast?
Act early. A soft read in week one means tightening response coverage, pushing the named swing deals, or pulling a campaign forward, with three weeks to work instead of a post-mortem. The forecast is a steering wheel, not a scoreboard.
Does it need my CRM data to be clean?
Clean data makes any forecast better, which is why this ships alongside pipeline management: dedupe, stage hygiene and stale-lead handling keep the model's inputs honest. The two are built as a pair during your rollout.
