How It Runs At Your Store.

During the build we plan the calendar with you, write the campaigns in your voice and define the segments from your real data. This is the campaign side of the house. Per-lead nurture lives in email campaigns, and the two never talk over each other.

  • Campaign calendar planned with you, then launched on schedule automatically.
  • Segments built from your CRM: equity positions, lease timelines, service history, past buyers.
  • CASL-compliant consent and unsubscribe handling on every send.
  • Suppression rules keep promos away from live negotiations and fresh deliveries.
  • Results reported by campaign in appointments and sold units, not vanity metrics.

Straight Answers.

How is this different from the email campaigns page?
Nurture follows one buyer: a sequence around their specific deal. Marketing automation runs the store's calendar: promos, events and incentives sent to whole segments on schedule. Same engine, same brand voice, different jobs, and suppression rules make sure a buyer never gets both at once.
Can it text promos, or just email?
Both, where consent allows. Each campaign picks its channels per segment, SMS for time-sensitive pushes, email for the longer story, and every send respects CASL consent and unsubscribe handling. The channel mix is planned with you, not defaulted.
Who writes the campaigns?
We do, with you, during the build, in your store's voice, and you approve everything before it is scheduled. After launch, the calendar runs itself and you review results by campaign in appointments and sold units. Your involvement becomes a planning meeting, not a production job.
