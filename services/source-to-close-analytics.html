Straight Answers.

How is this different from automated reporting?
Reporting is delivery cadence: the store's numbers arriving in your inbox on schedule. This is attribution: which sources and campaigns produced those numbers, stage by stage. The morning sheet says how you did. This says why, and where to spend next.
Do I have to change my ad accounts or website tracking?
No. Attribution runs on tagging at intake and the CRM's own record of what happened next. Your ad platforms keep doing their job. What changes is that their claims finally get checked against sold units instead of taken on faith.
What about walk-ins and phone-ups?
Tagged like everything else. Front-desk phone-ups, calls answered by Wanda and walk-ins all enter the funnel at intake, so the channels digital dashboards ignore, often a store's best ones, finally show up in the math.
