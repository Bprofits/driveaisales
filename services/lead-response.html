How It Runs At Your Store.

This is not a generic autoresponder bolted to your website. During your build we wire the response engine into your lead sources, your inventory feed and your brand voice, then tune it with your team before it touches a live lead.

  • Connected to your real lead sources: website forms, marketplaces, social pages and phone lines.
  • Replies written in your store's voice, approved by you during the build.
  • Inventory-aware from day one, so answers reference the actual unit the buyer asked about.
  • Human-in-the-loop: your team can watch, edit or take over any thread in real time.
  • AMVIC and OMVIC advertising rules configured in, so a fast answer is never a non-compliant one.

Straight Answers.

Is this just a fancy autoresponder?
No. An autoresponder tells the buyer you will get back to them. This answers the question: the unit, availability, next steps, in your brand voice, then qualifies and books. The moment the buyer engages, a human takes the thread with the full history. The difference shows up in your show rate, not your open rate.
Why does under 60 seconds matter so much?
Because the buyer who submits at 11:47 PM is usually submitting to more than one store, and the first real answer tends to frame the whole deal. We wrote up what the research actually says in lead response time in car sales.
What happens after the first reply?
Qualification, booking and follow-up. Budget, trade, timeline and financing get captured, the appointment goes on your calendar with confirmations and reminders, and anything that stalls enters a follow-up cadence instead of dying in the CRM.
