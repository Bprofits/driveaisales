A Library, Not A Script.

What ships in the library, and why it does not read like one.

Scenarios

The Situations That Repeat

Brand voice

Tuned Until It Sounds Like You

Auto-trigger

Templates fire when the matching situation appears in the thread. Nobody has to remember the right response at 6 PM on a Saturday.

Personalized

Every template merges the actual vehicle, name, appointment and history. The skeleton is shared, the message never is.

Compliant

Editable

How It Runs At Your Store.

The library is written with you during the build, scenario by scenario, then wired into the same engine that answers your leads and runs your follow-up. The result reads less like automation and more like discipline.

  • Every template approved by you before it goes live, and rewritable any time.
  • Merged with real deal data: vehicle, names, appointments, history.
  • AMVIC and OMVIC advertising rules configured in, especially around payments and pricing.
  • Triggers tuned to your floor: which patterns fire, which wait for a human.
  • Every sent template logged on the contact, so the thread reads as one conversation.

Straight Answers.

Will customers notice they are getting templates?
Not the way you fear. What buyers notice is tone and relevance: every message references their actual vehicle, their appointment, their question, in your store's voice. A good template is your best answer, delivered consistently. What customers actually notice is silence, and this ends the silence.
Can my reps still write their own replies?
Always. Templates fire where speed and consistency matter, and reps can edit, replace or override any of it, any time. When a rep keeps rewriting the same line, that is a signal, and we fold the better wording back into the library.
Why does the financing template not just quote a payment?
Because a payment quoted in a text, without terms and disclosures, is exactly the kind of thing AMVIC and OMVIC advertising rules exist to police. The template answers honestly in plain language and books the financing conversation, which is both compliant and, in practice, how those deals actually close.
