How It Runs At Your Store.

During the build we define the rules with you: what counts as stale, how long a deal may sit in each stage, which signals justify resurfacing a lost opportunity. Then the sweep enforces your rules every night, identically, forever.

  • Thresholds and cadences set by you, per pipeline stage and lead type.
  • Merges are conservative: uncertain matches go to a review queue, never auto-destroyed.
  • Revival touches run in your brand voice, through the same engine as your follow-up.
  • Reps see clear next-step tasks with context, not vague "check this record" noise.
  • Full audit trail on every change the sweep makes, exportable any time.

Straight Answers.

Will it change or delete my data without asking?
No. Confident duplicate matches merge with the full history preserved, uncertain ones go to a review queue for a human, and nothing is ever deleted. Every change the sweep makes is logged in an audit trail you can read and export. The rule is simple: reversible actions run, irreversible ones wait for you.
What counts as a stale lead?
Whatever you say it is. During the build we set thresholds per stage and lead type: how many quiet days on a fresh internet lead, how long in Negotiation before a nudge, when a lost deal is worth another look. Stores differ, so the rules are yours, not a template's.
Does my team have to work differently?
No. They keep working deals in the CRM as usual. The sweep works around them at night: cleaner records, revived leads in their queue, next-step tasks where deals were stalling. Most teams notice the difference before they can name the cause.
