Straight Answers.

Can I change what is on the report?
Yes, and you should. During the build we start from the fields above, then shape each recipient's sheet: owners usually want gross delta and pace, desk managers want deals and stuck flags, BDC leads want response and show rate. Same data, different altitudes.
Where do the numbers come from?
Straight from your CRM, the same records your team works in all day, which Drive AI keeps clean with automated hygiene. No side spreadsheet, no manual assembly, no version of the truth that depends on who built the report.
How is this different from the analytics page?
Reporting is delivery: the right numbers arriving on cadence. Source-to-close analytics is attribution: which channels and campaigns actually produce those numbers. The morning sheet tells you how the store is doing. Attribution tells you where to spend next.
