How It Runs At Your Store.

During the build we write the sequences with you, in your voice, segment by segment. You approve every template before it sends. After launch, the system runs the cadence, watches engagement and hands warm threads to your team.

  • Sequences drafted in your brand voice and approved by you before anything sends.
  • Segments built from your real CRM data: source, stage, vehicle interest, history.
  • CASL-compliant consent and unsubscribe handling configured in from day one.
  • Engagement routing rules set with you: what counts as a hot signal at your store.
  • Every send, open, click and reply logged on the contact in your CRM.

Straight Answers.

Is this the same thing as your marketing automation?
No, and the split matters. This page is nurture: per-lead sequences that follow one buyer through their deal. Marketing automation is the campaign side: seasonal promos, events and incentive pushes to whole segments. They share the engine and the brand voice, but one follows a person and the other runs a calendar.
Will this get flagged as spam?
Sequences go to people who contacted your store or opted in, consent and unsubscribe handling are configured in line with CASL, and the cadence suppresses anyone mid-deal or recently sold. Patient, relevant, personalized email to your own leads is the opposite of spam, and it reads that way to inbox providers too.
What happens when a buyer replies?
The sequence stops for that buyer immediately and the thread routes to your team with full context: the vehicle, the sends, what they clicked. Your rep replies as a person, mid-conversation. No buyer ever negotiates with an email robot.
