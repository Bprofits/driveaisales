Straight Answers.

Can an AI actually answer my dealership's phone?
Yes, and you can hear it before you buy anything. Wanda is our own intake line at (587) 525-7155. She answers in under 10 seconds, qualifies the caller, books the appointment and hands off to a human. What she does for us is what we configure for your store.
Will my customers know they are talking to AI?
For booking and qualifying, most callers cannot tell. She speaks naturally, handles interruptions, and answers follow-up questions. We do not coach her to claim she is human, and if a caller asks directly she is straight with them, because a dealership that opens a relationship with a lie does not keep the customer.
What if she says something wrong to a customer?
Every deployment includes human-in-the-loop oversight. Your team can review, edit or take over any conversation in real time. She is tuned to your brand voice, inventory and compliance constraints before going live, with AMVIC and OMVIC advertising rules configured in. If she is unsure, she escalates to a human instead of guessing.
Does this replace my BDC team?
No. She takes tier-one response, after-hours calls, overflow when every line is busy, and the follow-up nobody gets to. Your people keep the high-intent conversations and the closing. Dealerships typically move BDC headcount to higher-leverage work rather than cutting it.
